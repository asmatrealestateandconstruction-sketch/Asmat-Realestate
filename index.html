HOW WE BUILD

Ground to keys,
six stages.

The same sequence every project follows, whether it's a single home or a full commercial build.

01

Site & Survey

Land assessment, soil testing, and zoning checks before anything is designed.

02

Design & Permits

Architectural plans finalized and filed with local authorities for approval.

03

Foundation

Excavation, footings, and the foundation poured to spec.

04

Framing & Systems

Structural frame, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC roughed in.

05

Finishing

Interior and exterior finishes, fixtures, and final inspections.

06

Handover

Walkthrough, documentation, and keys delivered to you or your buyer.
